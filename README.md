## About Us

**Montessori College of Education (MCE)** is a teacher-training institution based in **Blantyre, Malawi**, located at the Millennium University Campus. Established in **2008** by **WAY Ministries**, MCE is dedicated to equipping educators with the knowledge and skills of the world-renowned Montessori method — thoughtfully adapted to meet both **Malawi's national curriculum** and **international Montessori standards**.

We believe in the power of child-centred learning and are committed to developing teachers who honour, understand, and inspire every child they serve.

---

## Our Mission

To provide high-quality Montessori teacher training that empowers educators to transform early childhood and primary education across Malawi — and beyond.

---

## What We Do

-  **Teacher Training** — Comprehensive Montessori education programmes for practising and aspiring teachers
-  **Nationally Aligned** — Curricula designed to meet Malawi's Ministry of Education standards
-  **Internationally Recognised** — Programmes benchmarked against global Montessori learning and teaching standards
-  **Community Rooted** — Founded and supported by WAY Ministries with a heart for the people of Malawi
